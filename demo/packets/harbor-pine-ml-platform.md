Hi Harbor & Pine Analytics team,

I'm writing about the ML Platform Engineer opening in Amsterdam. I build the plumbing that lets model teams ship without waiting on me, and your platform team's focus on self-serve training looks like the work I enjoy most.

At Example Studio Co. I own the training and serving setup for four small model teams. When I joined, each team kept its own scripts and nobody could reproduce last quarter's model. I introduced a shared job template that pins data snapshots and code versions together, so every trained model can be traced back to the exact inputs that produced it. Reproducing an old model went from a week of archaeology to one command.

Serving was the second problem. Teams deployed by hand and a rollback meant paging me. I set up a release path where a new model takes a slice of traffic first and gets promoted only when its live metrics hold steady against the current version. The teams now release on their own schedule, and I review the platform rather than each deploy.

I care about the cost side too. A monthly report shows each team what its training and inference spend looks like next to the usage its model gets. It's started more useful conversations than any quota ever did. Two teams moved their nightly retraining to weekly after the first report.

I'd be glad to share how I'd approach your feature store migration, starting with the parts that touch serving.

Best regards,
Robin Example
